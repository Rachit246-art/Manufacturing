CIRCULAR ECONOMY ARCHITECTURE

The Closed-Loop Metallurgical Lifecycle

From reclaimed alloy scrap to high-precision aerospace components, we keep virgin resources in the ground.

01

Scrap Alloy Intake

We recover segregated industrial offcuts, decommissioned pipes, and aerospace swarf, verifying metallurgical purity via optical emission spectrometry.

02

Renewable EAF Re-melt

Smelted in ultra-efficient electric arc induction furnaces powered by on-site solar, cutting fossil coke usage by up to 88%.

03

Precision Cold Working

Formed into tubes, coils, and plates with zero-water waste systems and non-toxic bio-based lubricant recirculators.

04

EPD Certified Product

Delivered to client construction and energy projects with certified Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) for LEED points.
