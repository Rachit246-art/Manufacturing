SHAPING INDUSTRIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Engineering Progress Through Innovation & Precision

For over a decade, INDUSTRIX has fused heavy industrial metallurgy with cutting-edge Industry 4.0 automation, delivering zero-defect components to critical infrastructure across 25+ nations.

OUR HERITAGE

A Decade of Relentless Manufacturing Mastery

Founded with the vision to bridge precision craftsmanship and industrial scale, INDUSTRIX has evolved from a single high-pressure tubing fabrication plant to an international network of robotic smart foundries and rolling mills.

Today, our operations produce in excess of 50,000 metric tons annually. We maintain uncompromising adherence to ISO 9001:2015, ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel codes, and stringent aerospace quality protocols.

2014

Founding & First Precision Mill

Commissioned our inaugural 12,000 sq. meter extrusion and laser cutting facility in the Industrial Corridor.

2018

Robotic Automation & Global Export

Introduced automated high-speed cold rolling lines and expanded distribution across North America and Europe.

2022

Industry 4.0 & Digital Twin Integration

Integrated real-time IoT acoustic and ultrasonic defect detection, achieving a 99.98% zero-defect production benchmark.

2026

Net-Zero Green Metallurgy Initiative

Transitioned primary smelting facilities to 100% renewable electric arc furnace operations with closed-loop scrap recycling.
