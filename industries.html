Powering Critical Global Sectors

From deep-sea subsea pipelines and automotive battery casings to pharmaceutical bioreactors and high-rise structural foundations, our engineered metallurgy meets the most punishing operating conditions.

Construction & Infrastructure

Modern megaprojects demand exceptional seismic resistance, tensile strength, and long-term atmospheric durability. Industrix provides heavy structural plates, tubular bridge piles, and high-tensile hollow sections tested to withstand high wind shear, seismic loads, and corrosive weather fronts.

As the electric vehicle revolution accelerates, light-weighting and crash safety are top priorities. SECTOR 03

Oil, Gas & Petrochemicals

Transporting volatile hydrocarbons through Arctic permafrost or deep offshore seabed environments requires metallurgy that laughs at hydrogen-induced cracking and sulphide stress corrosion. Our API 5L line pipes and ASME pressure vessel plates deliver uncompromising integrity under extreme hydrostatic pressure.

API Spec 5L (PSL2) NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 Sour Service Heavy-Wall Seamless Cryogenic Impact Tested
